About Me

Discipline, teamwork, and continuous improvement are values that guide how I work and learn.     Data, and how it can be organized and used to support decisions, is an area that keeps coming up while working on personal projects. Each project has added another step on the learning curve, with plenty of room left to grow.

    Breaking down complex problems and working through them step by step is a habit built over time through practice. Coursework and teamwork are good ways to keep sharpening that habit going forward.
