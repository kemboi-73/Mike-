About Me

I've always been curious about how ordinary things work, like how a payment goes through in seconds, or how an app knows what to show you next. That curiosity is what keeps me learning, one small project at a time, reading, experimenting, and rebuilding things until they make sense to me.

  • Web & Systems

    I've spent time with backend frameworks like Django and FastAPI, learning how web systems come together, from routing to data to the logic that connects it all.

  • Systems & Networks

    How applications talk to each other, through APIs, data exchange, and integrations, comes up in almost everything I build. It's a subject I keep wanting to understand better.

  • Data

    How data is organised and used to support a decision is something that comes up often in my projects, and it's an area I keep coming back to.

  • Problem Solving

    Breaking a complex problem into small, workable steps is a habit I've built through practice, and one I try to keep sharpening.
