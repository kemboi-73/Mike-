About

I grew up curious about the machinery behind ordinary things — how a payment goes through in seconds, how an app knows what to show you next. That curiosity turned into self-directed learning: reading documentation, breaking small projects on purpose, and rebuilding them until they made sense.

Discipline, teamwork, and steady improvement are the values that guide how I learn. I don't come to this with years of experience — I come with consistency, and a genuine want to understand things properly rather than just get them working.

I'm now looking to take that further through formal study, in an environment that can challenge how I think about technology and push me past what I've been able to teach myself alone.
